# Voice guide

Read this before writing or editing any page in `src/`.

## Register
- A formal teaching site written by a physician for faculty and residents. Plain and direct.
- Contractions only occasionally. Do not apply them everywhere.
- First person is welcome where the author has real experience. One or two short notes per module at most. No motivational lines.

## Sentences
- State the positive. Lead with what to do. Avoid "not X, but Y", "X is not Y", and "you do not need".
- No aphorisms and no slogan headings. Headings are labels ("Model choice"), and each page's H1 is its plain title.
- Vary the shape. Lists should have as many items as the content needs, so avoid defaulting to three. Pocket cards run two to four bullets.
- Leave out emphasis words such as "actually", "really", and "just".
- State each fact once, where the reader needs it, and link to it from elsewhere. No pull quotes, and no lede that repeats the pocket card.

## Examples
- Use work from academic medicine: building teaching sites and handouts, converting Word notes into visual HTML, keeping several documents in a project consistent, strategic planning, resident learning.
- Avoid generic office examples (spreadsheet formulas, marketing copy) and outpatient-only tasks (prior authorizations, appeals).
- Prefer a specific real detail to a general claim. Mark anything invented with an "Illustrative" label.
- Public teaching material is the default example for personal AI accounts. Grants, unpublished research, learner evaluations, and anything from a real patient belong to KUMC tools in every example.

## Terms
- **the model** for what you talk to; **AI tool** for a product
- **chat**, not thread or conversation
- **project home**, **project description**, **current state**, **approved outputs**, **reference files**
- Refer to modules by title, not number, so links survive reordering.

## Sentences that sound like the author
- "You have a very bright, always available, always willing to help, tireless instructor who can give feedback on everything, create multiple examples for anything, create quizzes."
- "Working on large projects, transforming materials into different formats, or strategic planning."
