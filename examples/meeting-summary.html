Worked example · Giving context

Meeting summary

A transcript plus the material around it becomes a meeting record in a format you reuse.

DraftThe prompt and output below are illustrative, with fictional details. Replace them with a trimmed real excerpt.

Goal
A useful record of a recurring meeting: decisions, action items with owners, and open questions.
Tools
Teams or Otter for recording, then KUMC Copilot (internal meetings are confidential).
Context added
Exported transcript, agenda, slides, and last meeting's notes.
Became
A reusable skill once the format stopped changing.
Key prompt
Attached: the transcript, agenda, and slides from today's education committee meeting, plus last month's notes.
Write the summary in my usual format: decisions first, then action items as a table (task, owner, due date), then open questions. Under one page. Flag anything on the agenda that was not discussed.
Output excerpttrimmed

Decisions: Simulation sessions move to the second Thursday starting next block.

Not discussed: Agenda item 4 (conference travel budget).

What I changed
Two action items have no owner. Mark them "owner needed" rather than guessing. Drop the attendance list.

What the surrounding material changes

Transcript only
Summarize this transcript in my usual format.

Decisions and action items, but no way to know what was skipped, and items already settled last month reappear as new.

Plausible, incomplete.
Transcript + agenda + last notes
Same request, with the agenda and last month's notes attached.

Flags the agenda item that was never discussed and separates new decisions from ones carried over.

A record you can trust.

Takeaway

The surrounding material does more than the transcript alone: the agenda tells the model what was supposed to happen, and last month's notes tell it what was already decided. Once the same instructions repeated every month, they became a skill.
