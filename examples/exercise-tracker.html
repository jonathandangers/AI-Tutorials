Exercise tracker

Build the tracker around the questions you want to ask, then let conversation be the interface.

DraftNumbers below are illustrative.

Goal
Log workouts in seconds and answer questions about training over time.
Tools
A custom database plus an LLM connected to it.
Logging
Dictate the workout right after finishing.
Questions
"How many runs did I do in May?" "Upper-body sessions this month versus last?" "When did I first mention knee pain?"
Checked
Monthly counts spot-checked against the entries they came from.
Logging, dictated
Three miles including warmup, average heart rate 141, knee felt fine.
Later question
How many upper-body sessions did I do this month compared with last month? List the dates.

Takeaway

The database structure is yours. Once it exists, both logging and asking happen in plain language, so you actually keep it up.
