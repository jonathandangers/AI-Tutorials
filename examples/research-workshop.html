Research group database

A Notion database, built with an LLM's help, that holds the state of every research group so meeting prep takes a question instead of an hour.

DraftGroups, names, and projects below are fictional so no real group's work is shown. Replace with an anonymized real excerpt if you prefer.

Goal
Keep 8–14 research groups' status, blockers, and next steps current without re-reading old emails before each meeting.
Tools
Notion database plus an LLM with the Notion connector.
Updates
Paste an email, upload a transcript, or dictate a quick note after a meeting.
Questions
"When did I last meet with this group?" or "Write a prep brief for Thursday."
Key prompt
Write a prep brief for my meeting with the Group C team tomorrow. Use their database page: last meeting date, current status, open blockers, and what they committed to last time. Five bullets at most, then two questions I should ask.
Output excerpttrimmed, fictional

Last met: six weeks ago. Status: data collection.

Committed last time: finish chart-review form; send IRB amendment.

Ask: Did the IRB amendment go out? Who is doing the second chart review?

How the database was built

I described the questions I wanted to be able to ask, and the LLM proposed fields that would answer them: group, members, stage, last meeting, blockers, next step, and links. I adjusted the fields, it built the database through the connector, and I loaded existing groups by pasting old notes.

Takeaway

Design the database around the questions you will ask, not around what feels tidy. Conversation then becomes the way you read and update it.
