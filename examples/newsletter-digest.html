Worked example · Scheduled tasks

Newsletter digest

The AI newsletters that reach my inbox, consolidated into a twice-weekly digest.

Goal
Stay current without reading a dozen overlapping newsletters.
Tools
An LLM with mail access, running on a schedule.
Does
Removes redundancy and filters out topics I do not care about.
Delivers
One short digest, twice a week.
Scheduled instruction
Twice a week: read the AI newsletters that arrived since the last digest. Merge stories that appear in more than one. Skip funding news and consumer gadgets. Keep anything about healthcare, education, or tools I could use this week. One line per story with the source link.

Takeaway

A good first automation: low stakes, reports back to you, and easy to tune by editing one instruction.
