Module 05 · Placeholder

Quality vs. slop

This module is not written yet. Module 04 asks whether the output is correct. This one asks whether it is worth someone else's time.

05

DraftPossible sections:

  • What slop is: output that is fluent, plausible, and generic, passed along without the sender adding judgment. It can be entirely accurate and still be slop.
  • Generic output versus work and understanding you could not have reached alone. Same tool, opposite habits.
  • Effort in proportion to the task, and respect for the recipient's time: review, shorten, and clarify AI output before passing it on.
  • A before-you-send test: Are the sources credible? What am I missing? Would I put my name on this? Would I say it on rounds?
  • How to spot it, in your own drafts and in what you receive: length without content, hedging, lists of three, no specifics, nothing only you could have written.
  • One side-by-side: the same email or summary as first-draft output and after real editing.
