Module 01

Start here

Three things to settle before you open a chat: how to approach learning this, who owns the result, and which tools may see which information.

01

Pocket card

  • Start trying things before you know exactly how. The LLM can teach you as you go.
  • You own the final product. Let AI do the work; keep the judgment.
  • Personal AI accounts: public information only. KUMC Copilot: the tool for sensitive information, including PHI.

Start trying things before you know exactly how.

You do not need to know how to build the whole workflow before you begin. The LLM can be the tutor and guide while you are doing the work.

Tell it what you are trying to accomplish. Ask it what to do next. Try the suggestion. Look at what happens. If it does not work, or you do not understand something, show it the result and keep going.

Iteration is part of the process. The first attempt does not need to be right. Your willingness to experiment, give feedback, and fix things as you go often matters more than arriving with technical expertise.

You own the final product.

Let AI do a lot of the work. Do not let it make the final judgment for you.

  • Do not accept a bad output. If something is wrong, vague, too formal, poorly organized, or simply not useful, say so. Keep revising until it is good.
  • Understand what you share. Nothing goes outside until you understand it and are comfortable putting your name behind it.
  • Verify what matters. Check important facts, calculations, sources, assumptions, and conclusions, especially when the consequences of being wrong are higher.
  • Keep sensitive information in approved systems. The same privacy and data-governance rules that apply to your other work still apply here.

Which tools can see which information

Before you paste anything, ask what kind of information it is. The tool decides the rest.

  • "De-identified from memory" is still patient information. Retelling a case you saw last shift belongs in Copilot, not a personal account.
  • UpToDate (through the KUMC license) is approved for clinical questions that include patient information.
  • Research with patient-level data stays in its designated research storage. AI work on that data goes through Research Informatics.
  • Unsure? Ask before you paste. KU's AI taskforce takes questions at ai_taskforce@ku.edu.
