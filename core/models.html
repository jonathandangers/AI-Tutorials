Module 08

Match the amount of AI horsepower to the job.

Most platforms let you choose a model and how long it thinks. The right choice depends on the task, not on what is newest.

08

Pocket card

  • Fast models for quick transformations; capable models for hard analysis and planning.
  • More reasoning helps hard problems and slows simple ones.
  • Watch usage if it matters, but do not let it stop you from trying.

Model choice

Fast, lighter models are often enough for quick transformations and simple questions. More capable models are worth using when the task requires difficult analysis, planning, synthesis, or technical work.

Reasoning level

When the platform lets you choose a reasoning level, give harder problems more thinking time.

More reasoning is not automatically better for a simple task.

Usage

More capable models, more reasoning, tool use, and large amounts of context can consume more of your plan limits. If usage matters, check it, but do not let optimization become the thing that keeps you from trying the workflow.
