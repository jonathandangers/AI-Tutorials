Practical AI Workflows

Created by Jonathan Dangers, MD

How to use an LLM to challenge your thinking, learn, get organized, and bring projects to life, while keeping your own judgment in charge.

Resident considering the AI elective? Start here ↗

Path 1 Start from the beginning Nine short modules, in order: the data rules, the habits that make output trustworthy, and how to run a larger project. Begin with Module 01 → Path 2 I have a project in mind Something you want to make easier, or something you have never been able to build. Start from that and pull in the modules as you need them. Bring your project ↓

Bring one project.

Make something easier

  • What work do you repeat that feels unnecessarily tedious?
  • What project are you stuck on or not sure how to organize?
  • What do you wish would happen automatically, or stay up to date on its own?

Make something new

  • What have you wanted to build, teach, or create but never had the time or the technical skills for?
  • What idea has lived only in your head?
  • What would you make if building it took an afternoon instead of a semester?

Then: check the data rules for what your project involves, and plan it with the six-step workflow.
