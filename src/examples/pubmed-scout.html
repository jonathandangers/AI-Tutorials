Goal
Know about new publications relevant to ongoing projects without searching every week.
Tools
Claude connected to PubMed, running as a weekly scheduled task.
Delivers
An email with useful papers, why each may matter to a specific project, and links.
Your job
Decide what is relevant and verify anything you plan to use.
Scheduled instruction
Every Monday: search PubMed for papers from the past 7 days related to each project listed in my project file. For each useful paper, give the citation, a two-sentence summary, which project it bears on and why, and the PubMed link. Skip anything that is only loosely related. Email the result to me.

Takeaway

The automation prepares an internal review product. It never acts on its own, and every paper it finds is real because it retrieved it from PubMed rather than recalling it.
