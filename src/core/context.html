Module 03

Give it the information a smart human would need.

Most weak output comes from missing context, not a weak model. Explain the task the way you would to a capable colleague who just walked in.

Pocket card

  • Say the goal, the audience, the constraints, and what the output should look like.
  • Add material directly: files, screenshots, transcripts, links, connected sources.
  • Not sure it is enough? Ask: "What else would you need from me to do this well?"

What to explain

Start by explaining what you are trying to accomplish and the background needed to understand the task. Describe the intended audience, important constraints, and what you want the output to look like.

  • Your goal
  • Relevant background
  • Intended audience
  • Important constraints
  • Desired format
  • Length and level of detail
  • Tone or writing style
  • An example or template
  • What you have already tried

The same request, with and without context

Illustrative: the same meeting transcript, asked about two ways.

One line
Summarize this meeting transcript.

The committee discussed several topics, including scheduling, simulation, and the budget. Members shared updates and agreed to follow up on open items.

Accurate, and useless to anyone who missed the meeting.
With context
For committee members who missed it. Decisions first, then action items with owners and due dates, then open questions. Under one page. Flag agenda items that were not discussed. Agenda attached.

Decision: Simulation moves to the second Thursday.

Action: Draft the new sim schedule. Owner needed.

Not discussed: Travel budget (agenda item 4).

Someone could act on this.

Ways to add context to your thread

Every item you add follows the data rules in Start here. A transcript of an internal meeting is confidential, so it belongs in KUMC Copilot.
