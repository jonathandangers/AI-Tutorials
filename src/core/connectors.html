Going deeper

Connectors

Pocket card

  • A connector gives the model access to one service and the actions that service allows.
  • Research connectors such as PubMed and Consensus search the literature, so answers start from real papers.
  • Connectors follow the data rules. A personal AI account connected to a KUMC system is still a personal account.

What a connector does

Copying and pasting works well when you are starting out. A connector removes that step: the model can read from, and sometimes write to, the place where your material already lives. You turn connectors on in the tool's settings, one service at a time, and the service asks you to approve the access.

With a connector in place you can ask in plain language for things that used to take several steps: find a file, summarize a folder, search the literature on a question, or add an entry to a database.

Connectors worth knowing about

ConnectorWhat it lets the model doA use in academic medicine
PubMedSearch PubMed and read abstractsA weekly search on your teaching topics, with links to each record
ConsensusSearch peer-reviewed papers by question and report study design and sample sizeA quick view of what the evidence says before you write a slide
Google DriveFind and read files in your personal DrivePull last year's lecture notes into a new teaching page
GitHubRead and update the files of a website or projectKeep a teaching site's pages versioned and published
Notion, mail, calendarRead and update notes, messages, and events in personal accountsA digest of journal emails; a project tracker you update by asking

Keep personal AI accounts disconnected from KUMC email, OneDrive, and SharePoint. Connecting them moves institutional information into a tool that is approved for public information only.

Building a tool that uses health system data? A custom-built AI tool needs approval from the health system's Executive AI Steering Committee before use, the same as a purchased one. Ask before you build.

Retrieval still needs checking

A connector grounds the answer in a real source, which makes invented citations much less likely. The model can still choose the wrong paper or misstate a finding. Open the link and compare the summary with the abstract, as described in Iterate and verify.
