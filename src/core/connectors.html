Module 08

Let the LLM work with the systems where your information already lives.

Copying and pasting is fine to start. Connectors and agents let the model read and update the places your work actually lives.

Pocket card

  • A connector gives the model access to one service and the actions it allows.
  • Agents such as Codex and Claude Code work directly with files and tools.
  • Connectors follow the same data rules. A personal AI account connected to a KUMC system is still a personal account.

Connectors

A connector gives the LLM access to a particular service, such as Notion, Google Drive, mail, or calendar, and to the actions that service makes available. That can let you find information, query it in plain language, summarize it, or update it when permissions allow.

  • Notion
  • Google Drive
  • Mail
  • Calendar
  • Research sources such as PubMed

Do not connect a personal AI account to KUMC email, OneDrive, or SharePoint. That moves institutional information into a tool that is approved for public information only.

Codex and Claude Code

Codex and Claude Code are parallel examples of another idea: instead of only talking about a project, an AI agent can work directly with project files and tools. That becomes useful when the work lives in a folder or codebase and benefits from editing files, running or testing things, and fixing problems based on what happens.

You do not need this for every workflow.

If your work lives entirely in Notion or Drive, a connector may be the simpler tool.
