Practical AI Workflows

Created by Jonathan Dangers, MD

How to use an LLM to think, learn, organize, create, and carry out projects, while keeping your own judgment in charge.

Bring one thing you would like to make easier.

  1. What work do you repeat that feels unnecessarily tedious?
  2. What project are you stuck on or not sure how to organize?
  3. What do you wish would happen automatically, or be easier to keep updated?

For live sessions. This block will come out once the modules stand on their own.
